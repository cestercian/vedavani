Need a person? Email developer.aktsk.yashaswi.kumar@gmail.com. Replies usually come within two business days. Tell us your iOS version and what you were doing when it went wrong.

What the app does

  • Today — one verse to sit with, chosen fresh each day.
  • Read — a selection of 72 verses from across the Gita, offline, with Sanskrit, transliteration, and English.
  • Ask — say or type a question and get a verse that speaks to it.
  • Reflect — write what you make of it; the app matches a verse to your words.

Common questions

The microphone doesn't work

Open Settings → VedaVani on your iPhone and switch on both Microphone and Speech Recognition. Speech recognition is a separate switch and both are needed. If you would rather not use the mic at all, tap the keyboard icon on the Ask screen and type your question instead — every answer works the same way.

It stopped listening on its own

A listening session ends by itself after 60 seconds, and also when a phone call arrives or headphones are unplugged. Tap the ॐ again to start over.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works signed out and offline. Signing in with Apple only adds cloud backup, so your reflections survive a lost or replaced phone.

I signed in on a new phone and my reflections are missing

Sign in with the same Apple ID you used before, then reopen the app and give it a moment on a working connection — backup restores on launch. If they are still missing, email us before deleting anything.

How do I delete my data?

  • Swipe to delete any single reflection or conversation in the app; the backup copy goes with it.
  • Delete the app to remove everything stored on the device.
  • To erase the backup and your account entirely, email us from your account address. It is done within 30 days.

The daily reminder never arrives

Check Settings → VedaVani → Notifications is allowed, and that Focus or Do Not Disturb is not covering that time of day. The reminder is scheduled on your device, so it also needs the app to have been opened at least once since you set it.

Which verses are included?

Version 1.0 bundles 72 verses drawn from 17 of the Gita’s 18 chapters — a selection of the most widely cited passages, weighted toward chapters 2, 3, 4, 6, 9, 12, and 18. It is a selection, not a complete edition. Every verse ships inside the app, so it works with no connection. The English is Kisari Mohan Ganguli’s public-domain translation (1883–1896), lightly modernised for readability.

Requirements

iPhone or iPad running iOS 17 or later. An internet connection is only needed for optional sign-in and backup.
