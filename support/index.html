What the app does

  • Ask — say or type a question and get the passage that speaks to it, retold in simple words and read aloud, in your phone’s language.
  • Today — the day’s verse, plus your daily rashiphal, birth chart and dasha periods if you add your birth details.
  • Read — the Bhagavad Gita, the Principal Upanishads, the Rig and Atharva Vedas, the Yoga Sutras, the Ramayana and the Mahabharata, offline and searchable.
  • Palm — traditional palm reading from a photo, read on your phone.

Common questions

The microphone doesn't work

Open Settings → VedaVani on your iPhone and switch on both Microphone and Speech Recognition. Speech recognition is a separate switch and both are needed. If you would rather not use the mic at all, tap the keyboard icon on the Ask screen and type your question instead — every answer works the same way.

It stopped listening on its own

A listening session ends by itself after 60 seconds, and also when a phone call arrives or headphones are unplugged. Tap the ॐ again to start over.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works signed out. Signing in with Apple only adds cloud backup, so your conversations survive a lost or replaced phone.

Why do answers come in Hindi (or another language)?

Guided answers are written and read aloud in your iPhone’s language. To change it, change the language in Settings → General → Language & Region. The app’s own buttons and labels are in English.

What are memory notes?

With guided answers on, VedaVani keeps a few short notes from your questions so later answers understand what you are going through. They stay on your phone. See or delete each one in the app under Settings → Memory.

I signed in on a new phone and my conversations are missing

Sign in with the same Apple ID you used before, then reopen the app and give it a moment on a working connection — backup restores on launch. If they are still missing, email us before deleting anything.

How do I delete my data?

  • In the app, tap the gear, then Delete all data (or Delete account and data when signed in). It removes everything on the device and, when signed in, your backup and your account.
  • Delete single memory notes under Settings → Memory.
  • Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device.

The daily reminder never arrives

Check Settings → VedaVani → Notifications is allowed, and that Focus or Do Not Disturb is not covering that time of day. The reminder is scheduled on your device, so it also needs the app to have been opened at least once since you set it.

Which texts are included?

The full Bhagavad Gita, the Principal Upanishads, the Rig Veda and Atharva Veda, the Yoga Sutras of Patanjali, the Valmiki Ramayana and the Mahabharata, all inside the app so they read with no connection. Every English translation is in the public domain; the app’s Sources page credits each translator.

Requirements

iPhone or iPad running iOS 26.4 or later. An internet connection is only needed for guided answers and for optional sign-in and backup.

Nothing leaves your device unless you turn on guided answers or sign in. Read the full privacy policy.
