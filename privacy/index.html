Privacy Policy

VedaVani — iPhone and iPad

Last updated 1 September 2026

VedaVani is a quiet place to read the Bhagavad Gita, ask it a question, and write down what you make of it. It is built to keep your reflections yours.

The short version: everything works with no account and no network. Nothing you write leaves your iPhone unless you deliberately sign in to turn on backup. There are no ads, no analytics, no trackers, and nothing is ever sold or shared with advertisers.

What the app stores on your device

Used offline, VedaVani keeps all of this on your iPhone only, inside the app's private storage:

  • Reflections — the notes you write, and the verse the app matched to each one.
  • Ask conversations — the question you asked, the response shown, and the verse it cited.
  • Your display name — whatever you type on the welcome screen, used only to greet you.
  • Your daily reminder time — used to schedule a local notification on your device.

Deleting the app removes all of it. Your name and reminder time never leave your device under any circumstances.

Signing in (optional)

You can use every part of VedaVani signed out. If you choose Sign in with Apple, the app turns on cloud backup so your reflections and conversations survive a lost or replaced phone. Signing in sends the following to our backend, which runs on Supabase:

DataWhy
User ID (a random account identifier)To label your rows so only you can read them.
Email address, from Sign in with AppleTo identify your account across devices and sign-ins.
Your reflections and Ask conversationsTo back them up and restore them on your other devices.

Apple's Hide My Email is fully supported. If you use it, we only ever see Apple's private relay address, never your real one.

Backed-up rows are protected by row-level security: the database will only return rows belonging to your own signed-in account. We do not read your reflections, and they are not used to train any model.

Microphone and speech

When you tap the ॐ to ask a question aloud, the app records only while you are asking and passes the audio to Apple's built-in speech recognition to turn it into text. VedaVani never stores the audio and never sends audio to us or to any third party. Only the resulting text is kept, as an Ask conversation, in the places described above. Apple's handling of speech audio is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

If you would rather not use the microphone, you can deny microphone access and type your question instead — the app is designed to work that way.

Notifications

The daily reminder is scheduled locally on your device by iOS. There is no push server, and we cannot send you a message.

What VedaVani does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No tracking of you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No selling or renting of personal information, ever.
  • No location, contacts, photos, health, or financial data is requested or collected.

Deleting your data

  • A single reflection or conversation: delete it in the app. If you are signed in, the backup copy is deleted too.
  • Everything on the device: delete the app.
  • Everything in the backup, plus your account: email developer.aktsk.yashaswi.kumar@gmail.com from the address linked to your account, or with your Hide My Email relay address. Accounts and all associated rows are erased within 30 days.

You may also ask what is stored about you and receive a copy, at the same address.

Retention

Backed-up reflections and conversations are kept until you delete them or delete your account. Signed out, nothing is retained by us at all.

Children

VedaVani is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, write to the address above and it will be deleted.

Scripture text

The Bhagavad Gita verses included in the app are in the public domain. The English translation is Kisari Mohan Ganguli’s (1883–1896), also public domain, lightly modernised for readability.
