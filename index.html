VedaVani

Read the Bhagavad Gita, ask it a question, and write down what you make of it. Offline, on your iPhone.

What it is

One verse a day to sit with. Seventy-two of the Gita’s most-quoted verses to read at your own pace. A question you can ask aloud and get a verse in return. And somewhere quiet to write what you make of it.

No account needed. No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your reflections stay on your phone unless you deliberately turn on backup.
