VedaVani

Ask a question of Hindu scripture and hear it answered. Read the Gita, the Upanishads and the Vedas. On iPhone and iPad.

What it is

Ask what is on your mind, aloud or typed, and VedaVani finds the passage that speaks to it, retells it in simple words and reads it to you in a calm voice, in your own language. A verse each morning, your daily rashiphal and birth chart, a whole library of scripture to read offline, and traditional palm reading.

No account needed. No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Guided answers are optional, and your words stay on your phone unless you turn them on or back up.
